Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Overview

Video Animal is a Meta Facebook App used to automate publishing and management tasks for Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts owned or administered by the app owner. The app uses the Facebook Graph API to publish Facebook Reels, publish Instagram Reels, and manage related page or account workflows.

Who Can Use This App

This app is intended for internal use by the app owner and authorized administrators only. It does not provide public login, consumer account registration, or third-party user access.

Information We Access

Depending on the permissions approved by Meta and granted by the account administrator, the app may access limited information required to operate publishing workflows, including Facebook Page identifiers, Page names, Instagram professional account identifiers, media publishing status, access tokens, and content metadata needed to create or manage posts.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to authenticate with Meta services, publish authorized Reels, manage owned Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, monitor publishing results, troubleshoot errors, and maintain app security and operational reliability.

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. Data may be processed by Meta platforms as required to provide the Facebook Graph API and related publishing services.

Data Retention

App-related records are retained only as long as needed for publishing, security, debugging, compliance, and account administration. Access tokens and configuration data are removed when they are no longer needed or when access is revoked.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect app-related credentials and publishing records. Access is limited to authorized administrators responsible for operating the app.

Your Choices

Authorized administrators may revoke app permissions through Facebook or Instagram account settings. To request deletion of app-related data, follow the instructions on the Data Deletion Instructions page.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the app administrator through the contact information listed in the Meta App Dashboard or the official channel associated with this website.
