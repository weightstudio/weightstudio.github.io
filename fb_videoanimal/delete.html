Data Deletion

Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Overview

Video Animal is used for internal publishing workflows for owned Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts. The app does not offer public user registration or third-party user login.

How to Revoke App Access

If you are an authorized administrator and want to stop the app from accessing a Facebook Page or Instagram account, you can revoke access in your Meta account settings or remove the app from the connected business, Page, or Instagram account settings.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of app-related records, send a deletion request to the app administrator through the contact information listed in the Meta App Dashboard or the official channel associated with this website.

Please include the following information in your request:

  • The Facebook Page name or Page ID related to the request.
  • The Instagram professional account username or account ID, if applicable.
  • A short statement requesting deletion of app-related data.
  • A way for the administrator to confirm completion of the request.

What Will Be Deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete app-managed records that are no longer required, such as stored configuration records, publishing logs, cached account identifiers, and access credentials controlled by the app.

What May Be Retained

Some information may be retained when necessary for security, legal, fraud-prevention, platform compliance, or recordkeeping purposes. Content already published to Facebook or Instagram must be managed through the relevant Meta platform tools unless the app still has permission to manage that content.

Processing Time

We will make reasonable efforts to process verified deletion requests within 30 days, unless a longer period is required for technical, security, or legal reasons.
